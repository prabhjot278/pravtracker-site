Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

PravTracker ("the App") is a personal food, gym, and weight tracking app for iOS. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Summary

PravTracker does not have a server, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with anyone. Everything you enter is stored locally on your device.

Information you provide

You can use PravTracker in two ways:

  • As a guest — no account, no personal information is collected at all.
  • Signed in with Apple — if you choose to sign in with Apple, we request your name and email address. Apple only delivers these to us on your very first sign-in; on subsequent sign-ins we use the values we already stored locally. If you choose "Hide My Email," Apple provides a private relay address instead of your real one, and we store and display whichever address Apple gives us. We do not receive or store your Apple ID password or any authentication tokens.

You may also manually enter information such as your age, weight, goals, meals, and workouts. All of this is used solely to power the app's features (e.g. showing your progress).

Where your data is stored

All data — your profile, meals, workouts, and weight history — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks. It is not transmitted to any server we operate, because we don't operate one. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

What we don't do

  • We don't use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't build any profile of you beyond what's stored locally on your own device.

Third parties

The only third party involved is Apple, via "Sign in with Apple," which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

PravTracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can sign out, delete the app, or use the app as a guest at any time. Since there's no server-side account, deleting the app removes all associated data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.
