A simple, private food, gym, and weight tracker for iPhone.

What it does

  • Log meals and track calories
  • Track gym workouts and sets
  • Log body weight and progress over time
  • Sign in with Apple, or use it as a guest — your choice

Your data stays on your device

PravTracker doesn't have a server. Everything you log is stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, sold, or shared with third parties. See the Privacy Policy for details.
